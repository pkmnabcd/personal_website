Research with the USU Upper Atmospheric Imaging Lab

Since the summer of 2023, I have been working with Dr. Yucheng Zhao on the ALO and ANGWIN projects. With this group, I have presented two posters and written a senior project thesis. I have written software in C++, Java, bash, batch, python, and IDL. The code that I have written for my research is available at my Github.
I have been spending most of my time at this lab working with our ground-based imagers of the OH layer of the atmosphere (about 80 km altitude). I have identified clean windows of image data (without twilight, clouds, aurora, etc) and used that data to investigate the dynamics of the upper atmosphere. The frequent time gaps in the dataset made it so alternative analysis methods were sometimes needed.
My first major project was an investigation of planetary waves in the upper atmosphere caused by sudden stratospheric warming (SSW) events. For this, I analyzed global MERRA-2 assimilation model data. This involved acquiring the model data from NASA, extracting the relevant data, and analyzing it to determine potential properties of the resulting planetary wave. For this project, I developed an admittedly clumsy system (I was young) for doing the various analyses in bulk. The results of my research can be found on the poster that I presented on it.
