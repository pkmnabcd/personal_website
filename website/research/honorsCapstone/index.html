Medical Physics Learning Project

For my USU Honors Capstone Project, I decided to dive into medical physics, setting the goal of writing a paper that explains the basics of the field in a way that anyone would understand. This was challenging in a number of ways. I first had to obtain a concrete understanding of the field. Then, I had to translate my ideas into a form that anyone could understand.
I spent many hours shadowing and conversing with a local medical physicist. It wasn't my first time shadowing one, but because of my physics training, I had a better grasp of the concepts than before, when I only had a vague idea of the field. I was able to witness all of monthly QA and some commissioning of a new LINAC. Sometimes while writing the paper, I would get stuck on attempting to explain something I barely understood, and the medical physicist would very helpfully explain the concept in greater depth while I furiosly took notes.
Going through this process while also reading medical physics papers, textbooks, and online resources pushed me to gain a solid foundation of knowledge of the field. Because of that, I was able to complete a paper and poster on what I learned. The paper is available here, but note that it doesen't include the title: "Principles of Medical Physics: Radiotherapy, Treatment planning, and Quality Assurance"
