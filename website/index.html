About Me

I am an undergraduate student at Utah State University. My majors are physics (applied emphasis) and computer science, and I am a part of the University Honors Program. I am passionate about physics, medical physics, and open source software.
I am currently finishing up my bachelor's degree and will soon pursue an MS or PhD in medical physics. I am currently most interested in computational physics for improving the simulation and execution of well-known and emerging radiation treatments.
During my undergraduate degree, I have been doing research with the USU Upper Atmospheric Imaging Group. I have also been working on a number of personal projects, including a monte carlo radiation dose simulation written in the Odin programming language. This website will contain information about my research and projects.
